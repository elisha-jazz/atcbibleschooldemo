Diploma In Theology

Course Descriptions

This Diploma course is designed to train the student to experience a biblically-based Christian life and to build a biblically-based theology. Interpretation and exegesis are followed throughout the learning process so that the student can carry them over into life and ministry. It also equips the student in practical evangelism and a clear understanding of world Religions and beliefs.

The subjects include Major Prophets, I & II Corinthians, Hebrews, Genesis, Gospel of John, Apologetics, Introduction to Evangelism, Pastoral Ministries, Systematic Theology III, World Religions and Process of Counselling.

The Diploma in Christian Service is a 64 credits, College Level course.

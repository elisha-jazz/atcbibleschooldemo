Bachelors In Theology

Course Descriptions

This Bachelors of Theology degree program is a 128 Credit Course, this will equip the student to discover his/her special giftedness and to be developed in careful preparation in accordance with God's Word and as empowered by the Holy Spirit. The student will gain deeper understanding and accurate application of Bible knowledge through the lens of the historical and Cultural contextual exploration of the Scripture, applying it in the local church and in the evangelization of the world.

The subjects include: Purpose of the Church, Principles & Dynamics of Groups, Study Skills, Research Writing & Communication, Christian Life & Development, Homiletics, Introduction to Missions & World Evangelism, General Psychology, and Strategy for Church Growth, Principles of Leadership Management, and Cross Cultural Communication.

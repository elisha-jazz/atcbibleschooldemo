About Us

Africa Theological Centre (ATC), formerly Africa Theological Centre for Evangelism & Missions (ATCEM), was established in Uganda in 1992 with a vision to equip believers who have a call of God on their lives to be effective ministers of the Gospel. The Bible School is an extension of Asia Theological Centre – Singapore (ATC), and is affiliated to the Victory Family Centre, Victory City Church, and UAOG churches in Uganda.

It is under the leadership of our mother church: Victory Family Center, A dynamic charismatic church with a remarkable vision for world missions. ATC seeks to impart vision for missions and evangelism so that our graduates will be prepared either to go forth as missionaries themselves or raise mission supporting and missionary sending churches. ATC is a learning resource where ministers x\can develop their leadership gifts of Apostle, Prophet, Evangelist, Pastor and Teacher and equip "...The saints for the work of service, to the building up of the body of christ..." (Ephessians 4:12). We are located in Kampala, the capital and largest city of Uganda where we provide a rich context of education and ministry experience for our students.

Along with spiritual and qualified lectures, trainers and helps, ATC Uganda has trained and graduated more than 1,150 students of different nationalities and various backgrounds of life. With training in Ministry Service (Ministry Training Program, MTP), Certificate in Christian Service, Associate Diploma, Diploma and Bachelors degree in theology many have benefited and gone onto plant churches and raise up ministries and outreaches in East, Central and Southern Africa.

Africa Theological Center Uganda (ATC) was established to serve as a training facility for continental Africa and the churches therein, training and raising up pastors, church workers and lay ministry in Biblical Knowledge and ministry experience.

MISSION

ATC's essential mission therefore is to provide, in a charismatic christian context, advanced educational opportunity which will enable the students:

  1. To develop academically through enhanced and deepened biblical and theological understanding.
  2. To develop personal giftings and ministerial skills in preparation for ministry to the Lord, His Church and the world.
  3. To enrich personal preparation for participation in the fulfillment of the Great Commision, propagating the Christian faith and planting churches.

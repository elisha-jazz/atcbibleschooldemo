Admissions

GENERAL

  1. Applicants should have a minimum of two years as a committed Christian and active involvement in a local church community.
  2. Candidates must demonstrate alignment with lifestyle standards guided by biblical principles.
  3. Enrolled students are expected to follow institutional regulations and guidelines.
  4. The Ministry Training Program focuses on preparing individuals for ministry leadership and church development roles.
  5. First-time applicants are required to complete an English Language Assessment.

GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

  1. Two passport-sized photographs.
  2. A one-page personal statement outlining faith background and ministry interest.
  3. A formal recommendation letter from the applicant's Senior Pastor.
  4. Photocopies of relevant academic credentials and certificates.
  5. Application forms must be endorsed by the applicant's pastor prior to submission.
  6. Submission of applications must be completed two months before the semester begins.

FULL-TIME STUDENT REQUIREMENTS

  1. Personal bedding items, including mattress, pillowcases, and bedsheets.
  2. Basic academic stationery such as pens, notebooks, and rulers.
  3. Personal dining items, including a cup, plate, bowl, and cutlery set.
  4. Personal care and hygiene supplies.
  5. General living items and appropriate attire.
  6. Provision for personal return travel expenses.
  7. Sufficient funds to cover incidental personal needs.

PROCEDURE

  1. Step 1: Academic Document Evaluation
  2. Step 2: Admissions Interview
  3. Step 3: English Language Assessment
  4. Step 4: Final Verification and Admission Selection
APPLY TODAY

The primary objective of the Africa Theological Centre is to provide comprehensive biblical instruction across all academic programs. The curriculum focuses on expanding theological knowledge while applying practical principles to daily ministry and community service.

Applicants pursuing Certificate and Associate programs must meet the following entry criteria:

  1. Completion of at least four years of secondary education (O-Level) or an equivalent qualification.
  2. Demonstrated capability to read, write, and communicate clearly in English.
  3. Completion of foundational discipleship or ministry training modules where applicable.

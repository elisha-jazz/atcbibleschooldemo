Welcome

Africa Theological Centre – Uganda (ATC U) was established in 1992 with a vision to equip believers with the call of God on their lives to be effective ministers of the Gospel. The Bible School is an extension of Asia Theological Centre – Singapore (ATC). The unique goal of the Africa Theological Centre is to teach you the entirety of God's Word.

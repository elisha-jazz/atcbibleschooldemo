About Us

Africa Theological Centre (ATC), previously operating as Africa Theological Centre for Evangelism & Missions (ATCEM), was founded in Uganda in 1992. The institution was established with a focused vision: preparing believers who hold a divine calling to serve as impactful ministers of the Gospel. Operating as an extension of Asia Theological Centre – Singapore (ATC), the centre works in close affiliation with Victory Family Centre, Victory City Church, and UAOG congregations across Uganda.

Guided by Victory Family Centre—a vibrant Charismatic church committed to global outreach—ATC equips its students for field service and missionary support. The center acts as a foundational training space where ministers cultivate leadership capacity and spiritual gifts, reflecting the biblical call in Ephesians 4:12 to equip believers for ministry and build up the Church body. Located in Kampala, ATC provides students with a well-rounded educational environment and practical ministry preparation.

Supported by experienced faculty and trainers, the center has equipped over 1,150 graduates from various backgrounds and regions. Through diverse academic offerings—including the Ministry Training Program (MTP), Christian Service certificates, and theological diplomas and degrees—graduates have gone on to pioneer new ministries, establish churches, and lead outreach initiatives throughout East, Central, and Southern Africa.

ATC Uganda continues to operate as a central training site for continental Africa, helping raise up pastors, lay leaders, and ministry workers through thorough biblical study and active ministry practice.

MISSION

ATC's core mission is to offer advanced educational opportunities within a Charismatic Christian framework, enabling students to:

  1. Advance academically by deepening their comprehension of biblical and theological principles.
  2. Develop personal spiritual gifts and practical leadership skills tailored for service to God, the Church, and society.
  3. Enhance their readiness to participate in global evangelism, sharing the Christian faith and establishing local church communities.

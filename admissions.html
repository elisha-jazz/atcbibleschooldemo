Admissions

GENERAL

  1. Applicants must have been a born-again Christian for atleast 2 years and be part of a minstry in the church.
  2. Applicants must have already demonstrated their commitment to the Lordship of Jesus Christ through a Godly lifestyle.
  3. Applicants must be willing to abide by the regulations of ATC.
  4. The Ministry Training Program is recommended for students desiring Ministry Equipping and church planting training.
  5. First time applicants will be required to go through English Proficiency Test

GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

  1. Two passport-sized photographs.
  2. One page written testimony of conversion and call to ministry.
  3. A written recommendation by the applicant's Senior Pastor.
  4. A photocopy of all relevant academic certificates.
  5. The application form needs to be duly signed by their respective pastors before submitting to ATC's Administration Offices.
  6. The application form HAS TO BE SUBMITTED to ATC's Administration Office TWO MONTHS BEFORE the commencement of the new semester.

FULL-TIME STUDENT REQUIREMENTS

  1. Blankets, Pillowcases and Bed sheets.
  2. Pens, pencils and other stationaries such as: Ruler, Eraser, sharpener, files for notes, if desired
  3. Cup, plate, bowl, water bottle, fork and spoon
  4. Toothbrush, toothpaste, comb, face or bathing towel ans soap (for bathing and washing clothes)
  5. Any other personal hygiene needs such as: shoe polish, shampoo, deodrants, slippers, etc.
  6. Enough money for transport back to home church.

PROCEDURE

  1. Step 1: Academic Document Evaluation
  2. Step 2: Admissions Interview
  3. Step 3: English Proficiency Test
  4. Step 4: Final Review and Admissions Selection
APPLY TODAY

Our main goal is to know God through His written and live word, which is the most significant knowledge in the entire universe. The purpose of this practical application of the understanding of God and His unchanging love is to serve Jesus Christ by serving others and pointing them toward Him.

Ministry Training Program

Course Descriptions

This program is offered in two modules of three (3) months each, it is practical, offering a range of: Inspiring, faith-building, and Biblical Studies relevant for personal. Pastoral, church, and community life. All designed to give Pastors and emerging Church leaders the knowledge and skills for effective and successful ministry life.
